Concord 0.63.0 fixes a flaw that let a member of a guild freeze it for everyone, so update every device, and anyone still on 0.62 should update now. It also gives pictures and files a proper home: a viewer that opens out of the picture, a Media panel for everything a conversation has shared, and an editor for a picture before you send it. The app has a new look from the lock screen in, and a moderator's device no longer lets a device you unlinked back in.

## Pictures in the conversation
<!-- hero: attach -->
<!-- headline: Pictures that open where they are -->
<!-- tagline: A picture grows out of the conversation into a viewer, and shrinks back into its place when you close it. -->

- A picture opens out of its thumbnail and closes back into it. Swipe or use the arrow keys to walk a message's pictures; drag down to let go; tap to show or hide the controls; double tap or pinch to zoom. **i** or **Details** shows who sent it, when, the caption, and the file's dimensions and size.
- In the conversation, pictures sit in rounded frames and load behind a shimmer. Several pictures in one message are laid out as one set of two, three, four or more, and a caption that only repeats the message is not printed twice.
- A spoiler stays covered in the viewer until you ask to see it.
- On a phone, pinching a picture no longer opens its download menu. Holding a picture still does.

## The Media panel
<!-- hero: search -->
<!-- headline: Everything a conversation shared, in one place -->
<!-- tagline: Photos, files, links and voice messages, by month, with a scrubber, pinch to zoom and select to save or forward. -->

- **Media** is a panel of its own: the conversation's counts, and a switch between Photos and videos, Files, Links and Voice. Photos sit edge to edge under month headings that stay at the top while their month scrolls past.
- Pinch, or Ctrl and the mouse wheel, changes how many columns there are. A long grid gets a scrubber that names the month you are at.
- Hold a tile, drag the mouse across tiles, or press **Select** to pick several. Selected items can be saved, forwarded or opened at their message. A forwarded item is sent again under the destination's own keys.
- Files, voice messages and links are listed with their type, size, sender and date; a link shows its site and title. On a desktop the panel opens out to a large view; on a phone it rises like a sheet.

## Edit a picture before you send it
<!-- hero: spark -->
<!-- headline: Crop, turn, resize and mark up -->
<!-- tagline: Fix a picture before it goes: crop it, turn it, make it smaller, or draw, point and write on it. -->

- **Edit picture** is the first action on a picture you are about to send. Crop it freely or to a square, turn it a quarter at a time, send it at 2048, 1280 or 800 pixels on the long edge, and mark it up with a pen, arrows and text in six colours. Undo and Redo go back and forth through every step.
- The edit happens on your device. An edited picture is a new image, so the camera details a photo carries, such as where it was taken, are not in it. Concord removes them on sending anyway.
- An animated GIF is not offered for editing, since only its first frame would survive.

## A new look
<!-- hero: profile -->
<!-- headline: A new look, from the lock screen in -->
<!-- tagline: Unlocking and first run become a stage, and the app's dialogs, menus and notices follow one system. -->

- The lock and first-run screens put the form on a panel beside a drawing of the Concord mark among its peers. Every warning on them keeps full contrast. The steps of a first run show as a three-part meter, and the recovery phrase's caution is a callout instead of small print.
- The passphrase strength bar no longer lays out the screen again on every keystroke. A field that is refused gives a short shake beside its message.
- Dialogs, menus, notices, empty screens and day dividers share one set of corners, shadows and spacing. Headings use Space Grotesk, which ships with the app.
- **What's new** is a deck: one slide per feature, read from these notes, with the fine print at the end. Arrow keys, swipe and the dots turn the pages, and in a right-to-left language the arrows point the way the pages turn.
- Every main screen was measured at five widths in both themes for text too close to an edge, overlaps and cut-off words, and every case found was fixed.

## Security

- **Update every device.** A member of a guild could, with a modified copy of Concord, send one message that froze the guild on every device that received it, and kept freezing it each time the device tried the message again. 0.63 refuses such a message at once and the guild carries on. Every earlier version has this flaw.
- A device asking a member to catch up is answered at a steady pace, so a member cannot use catch-up requests to wear a device down.
- Pictures you receive are checked before they are shown. A picture over 5 MB or 50 megapixels, or one whose size Concord cannot read, is offered as a file to save instead of being drawn. The picture editor refuses pictures over 24 megapixels and never makes one larger.
- A message is sent once. A send that looked failed but had reached the others is no longer sent again by **Retry**, by reconnecting or by reloading, and a disk that fills while a message goes out no longer leaves it offered for **Retry** and then shown twice on both sides. Pictures and files are covered too.
- Concord keeps its database's scratch files in memory, so a full temporary folder no longer stops messages being saved while the data's own disk has room.
- A device you unlinked is now refused by a moderator's device too, not only by your own. Your devices hand the unlinking, signed by your account, to the members they meet, and a member's device checks it before it lets anyone into a guild. A member's device takes such a notice only from the account it names, and only from an account it shares a guild with.
- A member's claim that a guild has moved on no longer makes a moderator's device turn everyone away at its door. The device still catches up.
- The app's page runs scripts only from Concord itself.

## Also

- On a phone, the tab bar and the channel list mark where you are in the accent colour.
- The quick switcher and the message menu take the new corners and taller rows.

## Known limits

- A moderator's device learns that you unlinked a device when one of your devices next meets it. A member still on 0.62 does not learn it at all, and could let the unlinked device back in.
- If you think someone has your recovery phrase, start a new account: whoever holds the phrase can make new device keys, and unlinking does not stop that. They can also sign an unlink of one of your other devices, which other members' devices would then refuse to let into a guild.
- A device still on 0.62 or earlier can be frozen by the message described under Security until it updates.
- The known limits listed for 0.62.0 still hold, except the one this release fixes: a moderator's device that did not know you had unlinked a device.

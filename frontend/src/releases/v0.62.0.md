Concord 0.62.0 lets a guild owner's device that was removed while it was switched off get back in by itself, cuts profiles down to a picture, a banner, a name and a bio, puts recent photos and files at the top of the attach menu, and closes holes found by a security review and by fuzzing.

## An owner's device removed while it was away
<!-- hero: rejoin -->

- In 0.61, a device of a guild's owner that was switched off when a member removed it never learned of the removal, and stayed outside the guild for good. In 0.62 it asks the guild's members for the changes it missed when it comes back, checks each one against what it already knows, finds the removal, and asks to be let back in, as 0.61 already did for a removal it saw happen.
- This needs the owner's device and at least one member of the guild who is online to be on 0.62. A member on 0.61 declines, as before.
- When a member removes a device of the guild's owner, the banner on that device now says it was removed and that it is asking to be let back in. Before, it said another device had replaced it, and no such device exists. The banner names the member who removed it when Concord can confirm who that was, and says "A member" otherwise.

## Profiles
<!-- hero: profile -->

- Avatar decorations, rings, card effects and card frames are gone. A profile is a picture, a banner, a name, a bio and the profile colours.
- A profile from an older version that wears a ring or a decoration is shown as a plain avatar. Saving your profile clears those fields, so older versions show you with none. Nothing is rewritten until you save.
- The presence dot is a quarter of its avatar's width instead of a fixed 14 px, so on the small avatars of the member list it no longer looks like a second avatar.

## Attaching pictures and files
<!-- hero: attach -->

- The **+** menu, and the attach sheet on a phone, opens with a **Recent** row: up to twelve photos, videos and files you sent or received lately, in conversations you are still in, newest first. The row is absent when there is nothing recent.
- Picking one sends a new copy under a new key. It does not tie the two conversations together, and nobody in the new one gets a key to the old copy.
- Spoilers, voice messages, messages set to disappear, blocked senders, hidden direct messages and guilds you have left are never offered.
- The row is hidden while you share your screen or while a guest is in the call. Only pictures already on this device get a thumbnail, and nothing is downloaded until you pick.

## Linux desktop without GStreamer's good plugins
<!-- hero: desktop -->

- On Linux, the desktop app's web engine crashes the page as soon as it loads a video or a sound when gst-plugins-good is not installed, and reloads into the same channel. Concord now checks at startup. When the plugins are missing, clips and voice messages show as files with a line saying why, calls are refused with the reason, and the camera preview, the QR scanner and playback of a fresh recording stay off.
- Install gst-plugins-good (and gst-plugins-bad for calls), then restart Concord. Every other build, and a desktop that has the plugins, is unchanged.

## Security

- It is harder for a hostile peer to confuse which account a device belongs to.
- A device you unlinked can no longer get back into a guild through your own devices' invite door.
- Imported history archives and chat exports are size-checked before they are opened, and an archive this version could not read back is not written.
- Imported chat exports no longer let a link or an @everyone hidden behind invisible characters come through live, and files reached through a symbolic link are refused.
- Out-of-sync detection is stricter: a device that is behind its guild notices it in more cases and asks to catch up.
- A busy guild cannot crowd out the owner of another guild who is asking to catch up.

## Also

- Holding one of your own messages on a phone puts **Edit** first. The desktop menu is the same list.
- The meeting dialog says that guests join the call as well as the chat.

## Known limits

- A device you unlinked is refused by your own devices, but a moderator's device does not yet know you unlinked it. Whoever holds the device could, with a modified copy of Concord, ask a moderator's device to add it back to a guild you are both in. A later version closes this.
- An owner's device removed while it was away gets back in only through a member on 0.62.
- The known limits listed for 0.61.0 still hold, except the one this release fixes: an owner's device that was offline when another member removed it.

## Before you downgrade

- 0.62 changes neither the keystore nor the database format; it adds one index, which 0.61 ignores. Going back to 0.61 loses nothing.
- Your profile saved on 0.62 has no ring or decoration, and 0.61 shows it that way.

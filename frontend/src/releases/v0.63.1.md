## Security

- On Android 12 and later, moving to a new phone could copy Concord's data, including your locked keys and the guilds' encryption state, to the new phone, although Concord had already turned Android backup off. Concord now excludes all of it from both backup and phone-to-phone transfer. A copied state could split a guild's encryption for everyone in it; the recovery phrase remains the way to move your account to a new phone.
- A device whose keys were saved by a newer version of Concord now says so on the unlock screen, instead of an error that read like a fault. Installing the newer version opens it; the recovery phrase is not needed.

Concord 0.65.0 rebuilds fingerprint unlock on the phone so that nothing in the app's page can read what it guards, gives videos and links a proper player, and stops telling your contacts every time you link a device. It includes everything planned for 0.64, which was never released on its own. If you used fingerprint unlock before, turn it on again: this release removes the old one and what it stored.

## Fingerprint unlock, rebuilt
<!-- hero: profile -->
<!-- headline: Your fingerprint opens Concord, and nothing else can -->
<!-- tagline: A key in your phone's secure hardware, released only by your fingerprint, opens your messages. -->

- Turn it on right after unlocking with your passphrase, or in **Settings › Privacy** with your passphrase. Your fingerprint releases a key kept in the phone's secure hardware, and that key opens your messages. The passphrase is not stored for it anywhere.
- If the phone's fingerprints change, Concord turns fingerprint unlock off by itself and asks for your passphrase. Turn it on again in Settings.
- Only a fingerprint (or face, on phones that count it as a strong biometric) opens it; the phone's PIN does not.
- Your passphrase still unlocks Concord, and you need it on a computer and to recover. Now and then, Concord asks whether you still know it. You can say not now.

## App Lock
<!-- hero: shield -->
<!-- headline: A lock the app itself keeps -->
<!-- tagline: Leave the app and Concord stays locked until your fingerprint opens it. -->

- App Lock is on once fingerprint unlock is on. When you leave the app, Concord stops showing anything until your fingerprint opens it again. A call in progress keeps its controls.
- Turn it off in **Settings › Privacy**.

## Videos and links
<!-- hero: attach -->
<!-- headline: A video plays where it was posted -->
<!-- tagline: YouTube links open in one box from poster to player, above or below your words, as you choose. -->

- A YouTube link shows its poster in a 16:9 box and plays in place when you tap it. Nothing loads from YouTube until you do.
- When YouTube cannot be reached, the poster stays and says so, with Try again. Escape stops the video.
- When you send a link, the chip above the composer chooses whether its card sits above or below your text. People on 0.63 see it the same way.

## Devices you link stay quiet
<!-- hero: desktop -->
<!-- headline: Linking a device no longer messages your contacts -->
<!-- tagline: A device your account signed joins without a word; a key it did not sign is still called out. -->

- When you or a contact links a device, it no longer posts a line in your conversations. The device's certificate is checked against the account's key, and the contact's details list it quietly.
- If someone you verified links a new device, their badge and profile say **New device since you verified**. Verifying them again clears it.
- A key in a 1:1 that neither of you signed is still a line in that conversation, with Verify next to it.

## A simpler sign-in
<!-- hero: spark -->
<!-- headline: One clear way in -->
<!-- tagline: The sign-in screen has one action; linking, recovering and new accounts sit behind one disclosure. -->

- Unlock, create an account, link a device and recover each have one screen with one main button.
- On the phone the button sits above the keyboard, in reach of your thumb.

## Also

- A reaction, pin or edit that is sent again after a failure (a full disk, a lost connection) now applies once. A reaction used to switch itself off when its retry arrived.
- Conversations fade out at their top and bottom edges instead of ending in a solid band above the composer.
- The command-line client can read and write chats: `concord chats`, `read`, `send`, `reply`, `react`, `edit`, `search` and `watch`, with `--json` for scripts.
- A device of yours that restarts and reconnects before the others notice it was gone now catches up in full.
- A guild you create or join on one device reaches your other devices even when the message carrying it is lost on the way.
- A pinch on a picture in a conversation no longer opens the message menu.

## Security

- **Turn fingerprint unlock on again if you used it.** Fingerprint unlock in 0.63 and earlier stored your passphrase where code in the app's page could read it without asking for your fingerprint, and App Lock could be lifted the same way. 0.65 deletes what it stored on its first start.
- On the phone, the app now refuses to start on a WebView too old to keep embedded pages apart from the app, and video and link-card frames run sandboxed with no way to open windows or reach the app.
- A notice in a guild is shown under the name of the member who sent it. A member could before make a notice read as if Concord had written it.
- Announcement channels and slow mode now hold for every kind of row a member can post, not only plain messages.
- Text from other people is stripped of control and direction characters before the command-line client prints it.
- Nothing of Concord's is copied to a new phone or a backup (from 0.63.1).

## Known limits

- Fingerprint unlock is on Android only.
- Going back to 0.63 after turning fingerprint unlock on: that version cannot open the phone's keys until you install 0.65 again. Turn fingerprint unlock off first if you need to go back.
- The known limits listed for 0.63.0 still hold.
